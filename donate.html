Integrity & Accountability

How We Steward Every Contribution

Integrity and Accountability stand first among our seven core values. Every shilling and every donated item is handled with transparent care.

01 • Budgeted Planning

Itemized Outreach Budgets

Under our Director of Finance, every children's home visit and dignity drive begins with an itemized budget tracking income, supplies purchased, and field expenditure.

02 • Direct Field Delivery

Hands-On Volunteer Delivery

Our own executive leaders and registered volunteers personally deliver foodstuffs, serve meals, and hand sanitary dignity packs to beneficiaries on the ground.

03 • Open Verification

Direct Confirmation & Visits

Supporters and partners are welcome to join our field outreach visits in person, verify deliveries, and request stewardship updates from our Finance & Operations desk.
