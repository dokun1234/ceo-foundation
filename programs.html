Mental Health Awareness & Safe Space

You Never Have to Walk Alone

C.E.O Foundation champions stigma-free youth mental wellbeing conversations and peer support circles led with Clinical Medicine & mentorship advocates. If you or someone you know needs immediate crisis counselling in Kenya, reach the Kenya Red Cross Toll-Free Helpline (1199) 24/7 or connect with our mentorship desk.
