About C.E.O Organisation, Vision & Mission

We bridge personal development with practical community service so that young people grow into ethical, compassionate leaders by uplifting others.

About Centre of Empowerment Organisation

Centre of Empowerment Organisation (C.E.O Foundation) is a youth-driven community empowerment and humanitarian organisation dedicated to inspiring personal growth, nurturing responsible leadership, and transforming vulnerable lives across Kenya.

We bring together students, young professionals, mentors, and compassionate citizens who believe that meaningful social change starts when individuals take responsibility for their communities. Through structured mentorship, mental health awareness, leadership development, and direct charity outreach to children's homes, we turn shared values into tangible human impact.

Shared Care & Human Connection in Kenya
Our Vision

An Empowered, Compassionate Society

To build an empowered, compassionate, and responsible society where every child has a chance to dream, young people realise their full leadership potential, and communities thrive in dignity and hope.

Our Mission

Mentorship, Leadership & Practical Service

To empower individuals, especially children and youth, through mentorship, education, mental health awareness, leadership development, and practical humanitarian outreach that restores dignity and transforms communities.
