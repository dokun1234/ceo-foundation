Handover & Zero-Maintenance Guide for C.E.O Foundation

Follow these 3 simple steps once to hand over the project so the organisation never has to touch code or pay monthly hosting fees:

STEP 1 • FREE HOSTING

Push to GitHub & Connect Cloudflare Pages or Netlify

Create a free GitHub account for C.E.O Foundation, upload this project folder to a repository, and connect it to Cloudflare Pages or Netlify (both 100% free forever with free SSL).

STEP 2 • CUSTOM DOMAIN

Attach Their Custom Domain (~KSh 1,000/yr)

Register ceofoundation.or.ke (via Truehost/HostPinnacle with M-Pesa) or ceofoundation.org (via Cloudflare Registrar) and point its DNS to Cloudflare Pages or Netlify.

STEP 3 • EASY TEAM UPDATES

Team Logs In at /admin.html

Whenever leadership changes or new outreach photos are taken, the Publicity Director opens /admin.html, uploads the photo or edits a leader, and clicks Save & Publish Changes.
