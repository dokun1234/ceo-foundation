Who We Are & Our Inspiration

Born From Resilience, Built to Uplift Lives

“Having journeyed through childhood challenges and experienced the strength and resilience that come with being raised by a widowed mother, I have transformed my past into purpose.”

Centre of Empowerment Organisation (C.E.O Foundation) is a community-centred youth and humanitarian organisation dedicated to creating opportunities where there are none, restoring dignity where it has been lost, and equipping young people to lead through service.

From children's home outreach visits and girl-child sanitary hygiene drives to structured youth mentorship and mental wellbeing conversations, we unite young changemakers around seven core values of integrity, accountability, and compassion.

Our 4-Stage Empowerment Cycle

How We Turn Personal Growth Into Community Impact

Step 01 Stage 1 of 4

LEARN

Personal Growth & Knowledge

Members gain practical life skills, mental health awareness, and structured mentorship experiences that build self-confidence, discipline, and a strong foundation for personal development.

Mentorship sessions Mental wellbeing dialogues Skills & talent discovery
Step 02 Stage 2 of 4

LEAD

Responsibility & Character

Young people step into real responsibility: planning outreach budgets, speaking with conviction, coordinating teams, and cultivating ethical leadership rooted in integrity.

Project coordination Public speaking & confidence Ethical peer leadership
Step 03 Stage 3 of 4

SERVE

Compassion in Action

Members step out into local communities, visiting children's homes, serving shared nutritious meals, distributing sanitary dignity packs, and walking alongside vulnerable groups.

Children's home visits Food & sanitary kit drives Hands-on community care
Step 04 Stage 4 of 4

IMPACT

Transformed Communities

Structured mentorship, honest leadership, and hands-on service create measurable community impact: keeping girls in school through dignity drives, feeding vulnerable children, and training young Kenyans to lead.

Restored dignity & hope Empowered youth network Sustainable community growth
